I am a PhD candidate in the [Laboratory of Vision and Digital Systems (LVSN)](https://vision.fsg.ulaval.ca/) at [Université Laval](https://www.ulaval.ca/en), advised by [Prof. Xavier Maldague](https://xavier-maldague.fsg.ulaval.ca/en), and expect to graduate in spring 2027. Before that, I was a Research Scientist at [Fraunhofer IZFP](https://www.izfp.fraunhofer.de/en.html) (2022–2026), working on 3D reconstruction, SLAM and scene understanding for industrial inspection.
I received my Master's degree from [RWTH Aachen University](https://www.rwth-aachen.de/cms/~a/root/?lidx=1) and my Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering from the University of Shanghai for Science and Technology.

My research connects **3D perception**, **vision-language-action (VLA) models** and **robot manipulation**. I am especially interested in making learned robot policies reliable: understanding why they fail in closed loop, and detecting and recovering from those failures.

## Current Work

My current research focuses on **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models**, with a planned submission to **IJCAI 2027**.

* **Reliable VLA control.** Diagnosing which delayed sensor input makes closed-loop VLA policies fail, and correcting it with a lightweight action-conditioned state predictor placed in front of a frozen VLA (paper under review).
* **3D world representations for robots.** Open-vocabulary 3D semantic mapping and training-free fusion of vision-language models with 3D geometric evidence for spatial reasoning (papers under review).
* **Real-robot manipulation.** An SO-101 arm with LeRobot: teleoperated data collection and policy deployment on hardware, used as a testbed for latency and failure-recovery experiments.
